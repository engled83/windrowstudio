Catching up with Devin

Devin Trout is a touring and session guitarist based in Nashville. A year after his first appearance on Look Who It Is!, he returns to talk with Dane about what has changed, what he has learned, and what he is looking forward to next.

What we talked about

  • Touring with Chase Matthew and opening for Jason Aldean.
  • Playing Crash My Playa and Meatstock in Australia.
  • Preparing tracks for TV performances and taking on more work behind the scenes.
  • Session work and getting ready to record new music with Chase’s touring band.
  • Building a live show, communicating onstage, and learning his role as a rhythm guitarist.
  • A beach adventure in Mexico, a tattoo designed backstage, and the rules of watching TV shows together.

About this conversation

We catch up on everything that’s changed since last year, including touring with Chase Matthew, playing shows around the world, opening for Jason Aldean, and taking on a bigger role behind the scenes. Devin talks about preparing tracks for TV performances, doing more session work, and getting ready to head into the studio as Chase uses his touring band to record new music.

We also get into life on the road, what goes into putting together a live show, learning his role as a rhythm guitarist, some unforgettable tour stories, and what he’s looking forward to next.

A relaxed conversation about touring, recording, growing as a musician, and everything that happens in between.
