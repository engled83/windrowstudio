About Windrow Studio

A small studio.
A love for music.

I'm Dane, the host of Look Who It Is! and the person behind Windrow Studio in rural Indiana.

The people behind the songs.

I started the podcast to talk with the people who make music happen and give their stories room to breathe. That includes artists, songwriters, session players, producers, and the people working behind the scenes.

We get into songwriting, life on the road, the moments that changed things, and whatever good stories come up along the way. New music is part of the conversation, too.

A conversation, wherever you are.

Most episodes are recorded remotely, which lets me connect with guests from different places. The format stays relaxed and conversational, with room to follow an interesting story.
